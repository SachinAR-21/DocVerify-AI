# Product Vision

For universities, employers, public offices, banks and landlords who need to check a lot of applicant documents quickly, reliably and in a GDPR-compliant way, DocVerify AI is an AI-powered document verification platform that extracts, cross-checks and verifies documents against official rules and creates an explainable report, so reviewers can decide in minutes instead of hours. Unlike manual checking by email and shared folders or simple OCR tools, our product explains every result with the rule it is based on, keeps a human in control of every decision and gives applicants direct feedback when they upload.

Main ideas:
- The AI recommends, the reviewer decides.
- No black box: every finding has an explanation and a source.
- Every AI decision is logged (prompt version, model, sources).
- Personal data is protected and deleted after a set time.
