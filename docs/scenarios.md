# Scenarios

## Priya uploads her documents

It is the evening before the deadline. Priya registers, creates an application for the Master's programme and takes a photo of her degree certificate with her phone. The system tells her the photo is too dark and the bottom is cut off, so she takes it again. Then she uploads her transcripts and her IELTS certificate and adds a note that her name is misspelled on the certificate. A checklist shows that all required documents are there.

Some days later she gets an email that her transcript needs a certified translation. She uploads only this document again. One week later the status changes to "Verified".

## Jana reviews applications

On Monday Jana has 63 new applications. The system already checked them and created a report for each one. Most of them are "Verified", so she looks at the extracted data next to the documents and accepts them quickly.

One transcript is "Rejected" because the font in two grade cells is different and the average does not match the grades. Jana agrees and asks for a certified copy from the university. Another application is "Needs Review" because the IELTS score is 6.0. She asks the AI assistant if the applicant meets the language requirement. It answers that 6.5 is required and shows the rule, but also mentions a newer TOEFL result. Jana checks it, overrides the result and writes a short reason.

## Tobias updates the rules

The language requirement for one programme changes from IELTS 6.5 to 7.0. Tobias uploads the new rules document to the knowledge base and archives the old one, so the AI only uses the new rule. He also adds a new document type "Portfolio" and gives two student assistants read-only reviewer access.

Later the data protection officer asks who looked at a passport and why an application was rejected. Tobias searches the audit log and exports all actions, including which prompt version and sources the AI used and which reviewer confirmed the decision.
