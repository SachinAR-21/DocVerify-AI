# Personas

## Priya Raman - Applicant

26 years old, from Chennai, India. She is applying for a Master's in Data Science at three German universities and mostly uses her phone.

Last year one of her applications was rejected because a transcript page was "unreadable", and she found out only after the deadline.

**Goals**
- Submit a complete application before the deadline
- Know directly if there is a problem with a document
- See the status without writing emails
- Be sure her passport and data are safe

**Frustrations**
- Portals that accept everything and complain weeks later
- No status updates
- Having to upload everything again because of one file
- Not knowing why a document was rejected

**Technical skills:** Good with smartphones and web apps, uses scanner apps. Not very familiar with PDF tools.

## Jana Becker - Reviewer

41 years old, admissions officer in the International Office of a university. In application season she checks about 50-70 applications per day.

She knows documents from many countries and can often see when something is wrong, but most of her time goes into routine checks and asking for better scans. She does not want a machine to decide about applicants alone.

**Goals**
- Finish her queue without overtime
- Spend time on unclear or suspicious cases
- Make decisions she can justify with the rules
- See document, extracted data and AI report on one screen

**Frustrations**
- Switching between email, folders, rule PDFs and other systems
- Bad scans
- Forgeries that are hard to spot
- Tools that give a result without a reason

**Technical skills:** Normal office user (Outlook, Excel, campus system), not technical.

## Tobias Klein - Administrator

34 years old, IT administrator for student services. He manages user accounts, rules and data protection topics together with the data protection officer.

**Goals**
- Control who can see and change what
- Update document types, rules and the knowledge base without a developer
- Know which prompt and model produced each AI decision
- Have a full audit log and delete data on time

**Frustrations**
- Tools where everybody is basically admin
- Old rule PDFs where nobody knows which version is valid
- Personal data spread over mailboxes and drives
- No way to export logs or statistics

**Technical skills:** High. User management, REST APIs, logs, basic scripting, knows GDPR and has a basic understanding of LLMs.
